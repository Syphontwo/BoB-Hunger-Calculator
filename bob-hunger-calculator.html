Simulation assumptions
At or above 35% food, drain uses actual growth. After a tick takes food below 35%, the next tick uses effective growth 1.32. Each tick takes 15 seconds; no other modifiers are applied (comfort, blessings, water, etc.).

Model, assumptions & limitations
Drain = (M × G_effective − 1) × (0.25 + 0.75 × Food_fraction)

Food_fraction is remaining food expressed as a number between 0 and 1 (even for Rex). On each 15-second tick, the current food percentage determines the drain; food is then reduced by that amount. Only after food falls below 35% does effective growth used inthe calculations change to 1.32. The calculator uses the precise unrounded simulated food value throughout.

The 50% marker is associated with growth slowdown with growth levels above 1.32. The 15% starving indicator was observed consistently across the detailed curves regardless of growth; health damage begins below 10%. This tool does not predict the time of death.

The combined equation and low-food growth clamp are experimental hypotheses generated by observation, not confirmed source code. Best-supported growth measurements are above 1.32. Juvenile behavior and special hunger capacities or species abilities may differ. Results may vary due to game-side rounding, mechanics, and environment.
